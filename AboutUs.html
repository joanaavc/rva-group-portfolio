THE IDEA BEHIND OUR NAME

WHY FIVE DIFFERENT STORIES?

One team can hold many perspectives.

We created this portfolio to introduce the five of us and share our skills, talents, projects, and experiences as students. Our name reflects both sides of our work: each of us has a story of our own, and together we are on one shared journey.

Through this site, we hope to show what we can do, what we have learned, and the work we create along the way.

HOW WE GOT HERE

A shared project, step by step.

Our starting point and progress, based on the team's assessment plan.

01 · THE CONCEPT

Find a story for the team

We chose a personal portfolio theme and the name Five Different Stories.

02 · THE PLAN

Shape the visitor's journey

We outlined six pages and chose navy, cream, and soft orange for a simple, welcoming visual identity.

03 · THE BUILD

Turn the plan into a website

We are developing the content and layout into a shared space for our people, practice, and projects.

OUR SHARED GOAL

Make room for every story.

We want visitors to meet our team, discover what each member contributes, and follow our student journey.
